**GIS in Motion** is the learning library of **Temitope Ogungbuji**. It documents a self-directed journey into land use and land cover classification using GIS, remote sensing and machine learning.

## Why this site exists

Learning a field like this means juggling papers, datasets, code and half-finished notes. Writing in public keeps me honest: every summary has to be clear enough for someone else to follow, and every claim has to point back to a source.

## How the content is made

- **Reading list.** Every paper is a real arXiv record. Titles, authors, years and IDs come from the [arXiv API](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/index.html), and each summary is a short note written from the paper's abstract.
- **Articles.** Methods articles explain workflows and cite their sources. They are reviews and tutorials, so they do **not** report new experimental results. Where results would appear, they either quote a clearly attributed case study or use labelled placeholders.
- **Books & texts.** Textbooks and documentation I am using or plan to read.
